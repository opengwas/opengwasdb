- **`build-ragged-besd` lifts each Analysis's Trait position with its variants (issue #433).** From an hg19 source, `trait_chr`/`trait_bp` were the source's GRCh37 coordinates in a Store of GRCh38 variants (APOE at 45,410,830 rather than about 44.907 Mb; 28% of a 200-Analysis OGS-00027 sample's Trait-chromosome rows fell outside `trait_bp` ± 1 Mb, 0.38% after), so a cis completion window sat off the gene. Positions now go through the variant lift's engine and chain (`lifted_positions`). A position the chain places nowhere, or on another chromosome, leaves its Analysis with no Trait position, never the GRCh37 value. Those Analyses are counted and named in the build summary and listed in `trait_positions_unlifted.tsv` beside the Admission record (cleared with the record), and a share past the variant lift's 1% threshold refuses the build. Nothing is added to the Store or its manifest. A GRCh38 source's decoded arrays and metadata are identical to before.
