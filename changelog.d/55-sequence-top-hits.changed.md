- **A Ragged release's and a Hybrid Overflow's Top-Hit Index is held to its planes as a Dense one is (issue #55, ADR 0074).** A sequence's tiers were checked by sampling: 1,000 cells per tier for existence, Z, SE and EAF, stopping at the first mismatch. Nothing checked whether a tier held every association that clears its cutoff, or whether its imputed flags matched the mask, so a tier could miss associations unremarked. The rules that check a Dense tier now check every cell of a sequence's tier too, and its completeness, over the same one read of the planes. The findings keep their earlier wording ("references missing association", "z value inconsistent with CSR", …). A tier missing an association above its cutoff now fails with "does not match stored z values", as a Dense tier does. Every published Ragged release (OGS-00001, -00002, -00006, -00007 and -00027) and OGS-00011's Overflow pass these rules in full, and their `validate` output is unchanged.
