- **`RaggedCSRReader.get_analyses()` and `RaggedCSRReader.variant_positions()` are removed (issue #351, ADR 0070).** Neither had a caller in this package: a batch of Analyses reads through `opengwasdb.associations.CsrReader.read(Cells.analyses([...]))`, and a variant's rows through the by-variant index (`CsrReader.read(Cells.variants([...]))`, ADR 0060), which replaced the whole-sequence `variant_positions` scan. Code that called either must move to those reads. `RaggedCSRReader`'s private `_span()` and `_pair_positions()` are now the public `span()` and `pair_positions()`.
