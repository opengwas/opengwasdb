- **The Association Reader reads a span of Analyses and windows of a sequence, and `RaggedCSRReader` loses its whole-Analysis and whole-sequence decodes (issue #393, ADR 0070).** `Cells.analyses(first, stop)` names a span of Analyses, which a Dense grid now reads (one read of each plane) as well as a CSR. `CsrReader.windows(region, max_cells=...)` cuts an Analysis, a span, or a whole sequence into bounded `positions` regions, and `CsrReader.z(window)` reads Z alone from one, replacing `z(start, stop)`. The query facade's top-hit scan, Ragged and Hybrid Reference Completion and the observed-view checks read through them, so every association they read meets the presence rule; their outputs are unchanged. A Ragged completion of OGS-00001 and a Hybrid completion of OGS-00004 are byte-identical to the previous build's, but for `created_at`. `RaggedCSRReader.get_analysis()`, `variant_indices()`, `z_all()`, `se_all()`, `eaf_slice()` and `imputed()`, and the `AnalysisAssociations` record, are removed: read an Analysis with `CsrReader.read(Cells.analysis(a))` (or `inspect`, which never refuses), a sequence with `windows()`, and an Analysis's variants with `structure.variant_index(*structure.span(a))`.
