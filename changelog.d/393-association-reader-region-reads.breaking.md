- **The Association Reader reads a span of Analyses and bounded windows, and `RaggedCSRReader` loses its whole-Analysis and whole-sequence decodes (issue #393, ADR 0070).** `Cells.analyses(first, stop)` names a span of Analyses, which a Dense grid now reads (one read of each plane) as well as a CSR. `windows(region, max_cells=...)` cuts an Analysis, a span, or a whole component into windows of at most `max_cells` cells: `positions` regions on a CSR, and `cross` regions of one Analysis's variants on a Dense grid. `z(window)` reads Z alone from one, on either layout, replacing `CsrReader.z(start, stop)`. The query facade's top-hit scan, Ragged and Hybrid Reference Completion and the observed-view checks read through them, so every association they read meets the presence rule; their outputs are unchanged. Dense Reference Completion reads its source's bands and each LD block's rows through `DenseGridReader.inspect(Cells.variants(...))`. A Dense completion of OGS-00008, a Ragged completion of OGS-00001 and a Hybrid completion of OGS-00004 are byte-identical to the previous build's, but for `created_at`. Dense, Ragged and Hybrid Reference Completion now refuse a source cell with one of z and SE missing, or a missing marker other than the canonical NaN, by the reader's presence rule: the error is `InvalidAssociations`, not `NotAdmitted`. Both are `ValueError`s, but code that catches `NotAdmitted` no longer catches the refusal. `opengwasdb.admission.require_paired`, which no completion calls any more, is removed. `RaggedCSRReader.get_analysis()`, `variant_indices()`, `z_all()`, `se_all()`, `eaf_slice()` and `imputed()`, and the `AnalysisAssociations` record, are removed: read an Analysis with `CsrReader.read(Cells.analysis(a))` (or `inspect`, which never refuses), a sequence with `windows()`, and an Analysis's variants with `structure.variant_index(*structure.span(a))`.
