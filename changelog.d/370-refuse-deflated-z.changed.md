- **The resolver refuses an Analysis whose stored z is deflated against its source's p-value (issue #370, ADR 0069).** Beside the median |Δ| rule, `EffectConsistencyPolicy` gains `max_median_delta` (default **0.1**): an Analysis whose median signed Δ (`log10 p implied by beta/se − log10 p reported`) is above it fails as a `controlled_failure`. A stored z carrying no signal (SPACox's `beta` 9.0 beside `se` 1000; a standard error holding `exp(SE)`) has Δ ≈ −log10 p, median 0.30 on a null-dominated study, which the |Δ| rule at 1.0 cannot refuse. The record's `diagnostics.effect_consistency` gains `refused_by` (`median_abs_delta` or `median_delta`) and its fingerprint binds `max_median_delta`, so `--resume` recomputes every record.
