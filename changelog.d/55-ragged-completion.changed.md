- **A Reference-Completed Ragged release's completion metadata is validated as a Dense one's is (issue #55, ADR 0074).** Two rules held only on a Dense grid. One requires each Analysis to hold imputed cells exactly when its `completion_n_imputed_total` says it does; that disagreement is the shape the ancestry-match filter's defect took (ADR 0028). The other requires `analyses.tsv` to carry `completion_n_missing_total`. A Ragged release's metadata could deny its imputed cells, or omit its missing counts, and still pass. They are now one set of rules for both layouts, and a Ragged release's findings name `data.zarr/ragged/imputed`. A Hybrid's Overflow is never imputed, so neither applies to it. OGS-00002, the one published completed Ragged release, passes both, and its `validate` output is unchanged.
