- **A Hybrid release's Ragged Overflow has its frequencies validated as every other component's are (issue #55, ADR 0074).** The `eaf` plane's length against `offsets`, its exception table against the cells the plane marks, and its decoded frequencies in [0, 1] were checked on a Dense grid and a Ragged release, but never on an Overflow. Its plane was read only to decode a residual `se`, so under a `float16` `se` nothing read it at all. A table that had lost a cell left that frequency to whatever the lookup returned. An Overflow is now judged by the same rules, and its findings name it (`Ragged Overflow eaf: …`). Under a `float16` `se`, a frequency that cannot be decoded no longer fails the `se` decode. The exception-table rule reports it instead, and says why. OGS-00011's Overflow (1.75 billion associations) passes these rules, and its `validate` output is unchanged.
