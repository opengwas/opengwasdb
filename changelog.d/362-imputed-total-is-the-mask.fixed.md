- **A completion's `completion_n_imputed_total` is the cells its imputed mask marks, a completion's `completion_n_missing_total` the cells the release holds no value at, and `validate` now says when either is not (issue #362).** Dense, Ragged and Hybrid completion took the totals from the sum of the LD blocks' `completion_quality` counts, so a cell two overlapping blocks both attempt was counted twice, and one whose fills they disagree on (cleared as `duplicate_conflicting`) was counted imputed though the release holds nothing there. Measured read-only on the published Stores, the excess was in OGS-00009 (2,514 of 2,514 Analyses, 798,831 cells), OGS-00012 (1,669 of 1,669; 11,681), OGS-00014 (850 of 1,053; 690,799), OGS-00015 (241 of 241; 276,932) and OGS-00017 (2,754 of 2,754; 68,834), 0.002–0.19 % of their imputed cells; OGS-00011's Hybrid was clean. The totals now come from the cells themselves, and so does the `eaf_scope` derived from the imputed total. A Hybrid's crossover takes each cell it overwrites off the totals once (it had put the blocks' sum back). It no longer rewrites a block's `completion_quality` row, which is that block's attempts, so it no longer fails with a negative count where blocks disagree at the crossover (spec §12 supersedes #307's rewrite). The new `completion.imputed_total` rule fails an Analysis whose declared total is not exactly its mask's count, and `completion.missing_total` one whose declared missing total the release does not hold (a Ragged release bounds it above). A Dense release's missing total now includes an off-panel cell left out for an SE above 65,504, which the release holds no value at, on Dense, Ragged and a Hybrid's Dense Component. `completion.quality_counts` judges a quality row as attempts, with a bound on each side; a Hybrid's Dense Component records the attempts its crossover overwrote in `provenance.completion.crossover_overwritten`. A total no rule can read (blank, negative, non-numeric) fails, in a 0.2.3 Hybrid's root `analyses.tsv` too.
